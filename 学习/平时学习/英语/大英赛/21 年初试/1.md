Report on the Added Value of China's Services Industry

    The bar chart below illustrates the annual growth of the added value of China's services industry and year-on-year growth from 2015 to 2019. In 2015, the added value was 349,745 million, with a year-on-year growth of 8.8% , and in 2019, the added value reached 534,223 million, with a year-on-year growth rate of 6.9%. During the five years, the added value presented a steady annual growth, while the year-on-year growth rate declined slightly and reached the lowest in 2019.

    The added value of China's services industry reflects China's economic growth. With the rapid development of digitization and the Internet in China's huge market, the added value of the service industry is estimated to continue to increase!

Both Mandarin and Dialects Are Important

    It seems contradictory when we mention mandarin and dialects at the same time because you either speak mandarin or a dialect. In fact, mandarin and dialects are both languages used for communication. Both of them are very critical.

    China is a great country with a history of more than 5000 years and has 56 ethnic groups. On one hand,
    mandarin is the standard language used in formal situations to lessen the communication barriers between people. On the other hand, there are many dialects of Chinese, including dialects of ethnic minorities. The purpose of popularizing mandarin is for people to communicate in a direct way and get the information expressed by others more easily. The importance of mandarin is evident. For dialects, they are a symbol of a certain region and several generations. Besides, they symbolize people's cultural identity and they are a a carrier of group memory. Every dialect is valuable and deserves vigorous protection.

    Mandarin and dialects are not contradictory. Chinese characters have multiple fonts. We attach great importance to this diversity. The verbal part is also spoken in various styles. These dialects are extremely important. While we strive to learn mandarin, we should also pay attention to the protection and inheritance of dialects.

- contradictory
- vigorous
- critical
- font
- ethnic
- inheritance
