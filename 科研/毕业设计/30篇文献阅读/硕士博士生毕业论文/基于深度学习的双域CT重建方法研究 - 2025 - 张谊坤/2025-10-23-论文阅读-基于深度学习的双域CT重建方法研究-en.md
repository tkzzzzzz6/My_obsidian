## 三、研究背景与问题定义

**研究背景**
- Low-dose, sparse-view, and limited-angle sampling severely degrade measurements and introduce noise/artifacts in reconstructions.
- DECT relies on expensive dedicated hardware (dual-source, fast kV switching, dual-layer detectors), limiting clinical adoption and compatibility with existing devices.

**研究问题（Problem Statement）**
- Under non-ideal sampling for CT/DECT, how can we jointly leverage projection- and image-domain information with deep learning and imaging models to improve reconstruction quality while suppressing artifacts and preserving details?

---

## 四、核心方法 / 模型 / 系统设计

**整体思路概述**
- Propose four dual-domain reconstruction/acquisition schemes covering low-dose CT, sparse-view CT, sparse-view DECT, and limited-angle DECT, imposing learning constraints in both projection and image domains.

**方法或系统结构**
- Low-dose CT: dual-domain generator + adversarial learning.
- Sparse-view CT: cascaded projection inpainting, residual awareness, and image enhancement modules in a dual-domain iterative framework.
- Sparse-view DECT: complementary sparse scanning via slow kV switching + dual-domain joint learning.
- Limited-angle DECT: dual-quarter scan acquisition + dual-domain prior enhanced deep iterative reconstruction, with adversarial learning for visual enhancement.

**关键算法 / 技术点**
- Dual-domain generator and dual-domain loss (low-dose CT)
- Deep unrolling with projection/residual/image constraints
- Dual-stream ResUNet (shared encoder + energy-specific encoders) for DECT
- Dual-domain priors + dense cross-iteration connections to stabilize training

## 六、实验结果与分析

**主要实验结果**
- Low-dose CT: better noise suppression, tiny structure restoration, and visual quality.
- Sparse-view CT: progressive improvement with stronger artifact removal and detail recovery.
- Sparse-view DECT: improved streaking artifact removal and fine feature restoration.
- Limited-angle DECT: better wedge artifact removal, HU correction, and detail recovery.

## 七、创新点与贡献总结

**主要创新点**
- Dual-domain generator + adversarial learning for low-dose CT reconstruction
- Dual deep regularizers enhanced deep iterative reconstruction for sparse-view CT
- Complementary sparse scanning for DECT with dual-domain joint learning
- Dual-domain prior enhanced deep iterative reconstruction + GAN for limited-angle DECT

**论文贡献**
- A coherent dual-domain reconstruction and acquisition framework across CT/DECT degradation scenarios.

## 八、局限性与改进空间

**论文局限性**
- Dependence on training data distribution and limited cross-dataset generalization.
- Convergence theory for deep iterative models is hard to guarantee.
- High engineering complexity; real-time clinical deployment remains challenging.

**潜在改进方向**
- Add self-supervision or physics-consistency constraints to reduce data dependency.
- Explore global modeling (e.g., Transformers).
- Optimize inference speed for clinical use.
