1) Small: Due to the long imaging distance, infrared targets are generally small, ranging from one pixel to tens of pixels in the images. 
2) Dim: Infrared targets usually have low signal-to-clutter ratio (SCR) and are easily immersed in heavy noise and clutter background. 
3) Shapeless: Infrared small targets have limited shape characteristics.
4) Changeable: The sizes and shapes of infrared targets vary a lot among different scenarios.
